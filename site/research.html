The three carbon analyses a cement plant's QC lab does not produce

XRF, XRD, and calorimetry control product and fuel value. None of them yields the biogenic fraction, the non-carbonate carbon, or the calcination degree of the dust that leaves the kiln. The note locates where the real uncertainty sits and names the standards that close it.

The 2027 verification window

First-year site visits are mandatory, 2026 emissions cannot be verified until 2027, and the accredited-verifier pool holds no accredited CBAM verifier yet. On that schedule, readiness becomes a 2026 problem rather than a 2027 one.
