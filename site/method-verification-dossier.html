Verification dossier

The dossier assembles the evidence an accredited verifier requires. Strata builds it to that handoff from the first day of monitoring rather than reconstructing it at the end.

What the dossier holds

Verification is not a formality bolted on once the figure is computed. A verifier tests the method, the data trail, and the controls for the risk of material misstatement. The dossier holds the assembled evidence that lets a verifier do so without rebuilding the work.

  • The method and its justification. The monitoring plan, the chosen methods and tiers per stream, and the reasoning behind each selection.
  • The data trail. Activity data, analyses, and laboratory reports traced reproducibly from raw measurement to reported figure.
  • The QA and control evidence. The data-flow activities and control system a verifier examines for the risk of misstatement.
  • The uncertainty assessment. Each term's tier and uncertainty, together with the propagated result.

Built to the handoff

  • To the CBAM verification rules. Verification of CBAM embedded-emissions data is carried out under Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2546, by verifiers accredited under Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2551, on the model of the AVR, Reg. (EU) 2018/2067. The dossier is assembled to those rules and the verifier's accreditation requirements, so the evidence matches what the opinion is issued against.
  • For the first-year site visit. Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2546 requires a physical site visit in the first year of verification. The practice prepares the dossier and the site for that visit together.
  • Managed, not performed. Strata prepares the dossier and manages the verifier engagement. The verification opinion is issued by the independent accredited body.

The deliverable

The engagement produces a verification dossier assembled to the CBAM verification rules and support through the verifier's review. It is a complete evidence package, delivered alongside a managed handoff to the accredited verifier who signs it.

Prepared to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2546, on the model of the AVR, Reg. (EU) 2018/2067; verifiers accredited under Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2551, Reg. (EC) 765/2008 and the ISO 14065 / ISO 17029 family. The verification opinion is issued by the independent accredited verifier, not by Strata.
