Strata/About

A measurement practice, built to the rules it reports to.

Strata is an embedded-emissions measurement and MRV engineering practice. It prepares the emissions data that producers exposed to the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism are required to report. Each figure is determined at the source-stream level, documented to the EU monitoring rules, and built to pass independent verification. Strata is a newly established practice based in Berlin.

What the practice is

Strata prepares and integrates emissions data for industrial installations exposed to CBAM. The work is measurement and documentation. The practice defines what is monitored and to what tier, runs the analyses that close the carbon balance, determines the figure per good, and assembles the evidence a verifier needs. Specialist analysis such as radiocarbon for the biogenic fraction and elemental analysis for organic carbon is run through accredited laboratories. Strata integrates those results into a determination that holds together end to end.

An engineering discipline

CBAM is a measurement problem before it is a sustainability one. The deliverable is a number carrying a method and an error bar. Strata speaks in named deliverables, cited standards, and stated uncertainty, the register a verifier or a plant engineer can check. A figure that cannot be checked has little value in a report.

The boundary

Strata prepares and documents emissions data and does not issue verification opinions. The opinion is issued by an independent body accredited under Reg. (EC) 765/2008 and the AVR (Reg. (EU) 2018/2067). The practice builds to that handoff and manages it. Strata does not describe itself as accredited, as a notified body, or as a verifier. Those are reserved terms with a specific legal meaning, and loose use of them is the first sign a preparer does not understand the regime.

The standards the work is held to

Monitoring and reporting to Reg. (EU) 2018/2066 (MRR) and the CBAM definitive acts; verification to Reg. (EU) 2018/2067 (AVR) under Reg. (EC) 765/2008 and the ISO 14065 / ISO 17029 family; carbon-specific analyses to EN ISO 13833 and EN ISO 21644 (biogenic ¹⁴C), EN 13137 and DIN 19539 (organic carbon), under ISO 17025 laboratory accreditation.

How the practice publishes

A practice this young earns trust by being correct in public before it has a history to be trusted on. Its working notes set out the method and read the regime as the rules land. The notes are open, dated, and cited. They stand as the practice's argument that it knows the work, made where anyone can check it.

Get in touch

Strata takes on installations from the goods in scope (CN codes), the installation itself, and the monitoring documentation held to date. From these it produces a readiness assessment against the MRR that sets out the source streams in scope, the tier each must reach, and the gaps to close before the reporting period. Strata prepares and documents the data, and the verification opinion is issued by an independent accredited verifier. The practice is based in Berlin and is reached at hello@example.com.
