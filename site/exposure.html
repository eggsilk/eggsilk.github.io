Basis and limits of the figure

The figure is an indicative estimate. It does not constitute legal, tax, or financial advice. Any decision should be checked against EU Regulation (EU) 2023/956 and Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2621.

The default and intensity values shown are sector-typical placeholders. Actual CBAM default values are country- and product-specific and are updated by the Commission. The figures here may lag the official tables and are being finalised against them.

The certificate price varies, quarterly in 2026 and weekly from 2027, while the estimate uses one fixed price. The estimate assumes no carbon price is paid in the country of origin. A saving requires emissions data that is third-party verified and accepted under CBAM. Where actuals are unverified they cannot be used, and the default plus mark-up applies instead. For iron and steel, aluminium, and hydrogen only direct emissions carry a certificate obligation in the definitive period, so preset intensities that include indirect emissions overstate the chargeable figure for those goods.
