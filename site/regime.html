The CBAM regime

A working guide to the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism, written for the two parties it binds: the EU importer who carries the obligation, and the non-EU producer whose goods and data are in scope. Each entry answers one question. The core set is published first, and the guide grows from there.

The regime at a glance
Definitive periodBegins 2026. The first declaration and certificate surrender fall due 30 September 2027.
Covered goodsCement, steel, aluminium, fertiliser, hydrogen, and electricity.
The numberEmbedded emissions per tonne of good, either measured and verified or assigned a default value.
Default mark-up10%, 20%, 30% for 2026, 2027, 2028 onward (cement, steel, aluminium); 1% for fertilisers.
PricingCBAM certificates track the EU ETS price. Free ETS allocation phases out through 2034.
