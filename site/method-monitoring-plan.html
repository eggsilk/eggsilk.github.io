Monitoring plan

The document defines what is measured, by what method, and to what tier. It is approved before a reporting period begins, and it is the baseline on which every later deliverable is built.

What the plan fixes

Before a single tonne of CO₂ is counted, the plan settles three things: what is in scope, how each part is measured, and to what precision. When these are set wrong, every later number inherits the error.

  • The installation boundary. Every source stream (fuels, raw materials, process inputs) and every emission source in scope, each classified major, minor, or de-minimis by its share of total emissions. That classification sets how much rigour a stream is owed.
  • The method per stream. Calculation-based (standard or mass-balance) or measurement-based, chosen per source stream rather than per installation. A kiln and its fuels are rarely best served by one approach.
  • The required tier per stream. Set by the installation's emission category and the permitted uncertainty on activity data and emission factors. Annex II runs from ±7.5% at Tier 1 to ±1.5% at Tier 4, the bar each stream's measurement must clear.

How it is built

  • To the structure the regulation prescribes. Articles 12 and 14 and the Annex I template govern the layout, so the plan maps onto what an approving authority and a verifier expect to find, in the order they expect it.
  • Reconciled against the installation as built. Source streams are checked against the site's actual metering and mass-balance points, so the plan reflects how the plant runs rather than how a flowsheet drew it.
  • With the data flow and controls documented alongside. The data-flow activities and the control system keep the figure reproducible between reporting periods. This is the part a verifier tests, and the part that fails silently when it is left implicit.

The deliverable

The deliverable is an approval-ready monitoring plan in the Annex I structure, with the supporting data-flow and control procedures. It is the document an installation submits for approval. It is also the baseline on which the measurement campaign, the emissions determination, and the verification dossier are all built.

Prepared to the EU Monitoring and Reporting Regulation, Reg. (EU) 2018/2066: Articles 12 and 14 (the monitoring plan and its modification), Annex I (plan content), and Annex II (tiers and uncertainty).
