Emissions determination

The measured streams become an embedded-emissions figure per good and per CN code, and the uncertainty budget is stated alongside it.

What is computed

Total emissions form a sum of independently determined terms rather than a single quantity. The practice decomposes them, computes each by its prescribed method, and attributes the result to the goods that carry it.

  • Calcination CO₂. The carbonate decomposition the kiln runs on, namely CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ (0.785 t CO₂ per tonne CaO) and MgCO₃ → MgO + CO₂ (1.092 per tonne MgO). Where installation-specific factors are not yet available, the MRR Tier 1 clinker default of 0.525 t CO₂ per tonne applies (Annex IV §9).
  • Dust calcination. The CO₂ that leaves with cement kiln dust, added back as a separate source with EF_CKD = EF_Cli × d, where d is the measured degree of calcination.
  • Fuel combustion, net of biomass. Combustion CO₂ with the biogenic fraction zero-rated, so only fossil carbon is reported.
  • Non-carbonate carbon. The organic-carbon term oxidised to CO₂, added as a process emission per Annex IV.

How it is determined

  • By the mass-balance or standard method. The method is chosen per source stream under MRR Articles 24 to 25 rather than imposed uniformly across the installation.
  • Attributed per good and per CN code. Clinker is treated as a simple good and cement as a complex good that rolls up its precursors, so the figure maps onto what a CBAM declaration reports.
  • With an uncertainty budget per stream. Each term carries its tier and uncertainty (Annex II: ±7.5% to ±1.5%) and is then propagated, so the figure arrives with its error bar rather than as false precision.

The deliverable

The engagement produces an embedded-emissions figure per good and CN code, together with the full calculation trail and the uncertainty budget that supports it. This determined result is what the verification dossier is later assembled around.

Prepared to the EU Monitoring and Reporting Regulation, Reg. (EU) 2018/2066: Articles 24 to 25 (calculation methods), Annex IV §9 (cement clinker), Annex II (tiers and uncertainty).

Stoichiometric factors and the Tier 1 default are cited as they stand in the consolidated MRR; specific clause numbers and any installation-specific factors are confirmed against the primary text before they inform an engagement.
